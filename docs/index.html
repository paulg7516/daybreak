How it works

They tag it. You just clear it.

No digging, no guessing - each message lands already sorted by what it needs from you.

1

Someone tags it, in Outlook

When a colleague emails you, they open Daybreak in the compose window and mark what they need - with an optional deadline. The tag travels with the message.

DecisionInputFYI
2

It lands in your queue

Only tagged messages show up, grouped by what's being asked of you. No inference, no noise - nothing you weren't tagged on.

3

You clear it in a click

Sorted by urgency. Open the email right in Outlook, mark it done, move on. The queue lives in a pinned side panel, always a click away.

Why Daybreak

Not another inbox. A shortlist of what actually needs you.

Declared, not guessed

Focused Inbox and AI guess what matters. Daybreak knows - because the sender told it. No false positives, nothing important buried.

Lives in Outlook

A pinned side panel, not another app to install, check and forget. Tag when you send, triage when you read - all in one place.

Three clear lanes

Decision, Input, FYI. Every item lands in exactly one, so you always know the next move - and urgency badges float what's due to the top.

Your queue is yours

Everyone sees only their own tagged mail, read straight from Microsoft 365. Daybreak runs with no server - nothing about your queue is stored anywhere.

Open, done, gone

Click a row to open the real email in Outlook. Mark it done and it leaves the queue. When it's empty, it just says so - honestly.

One admin deploy

Your Microsoft 365 admin rolls it out once. Everyone gets the tag button and their own queue - no per-person installs, no downloads.

The first day back

Back from leave? Skip the archaeology.

Return from a week off to hundreds of unread, and the two things that actually needed you are buried on page four. Daybreak floats them to the top of your queue - so you spot what needs you, deal with it first, and skip digging through the rest.

The three lanes

Every ask is one of three things

Urgency is a separate badge - Overdue, Today, This week - so the lane always answers "what kind of ask," never "how loud."

Needs your decision

You're the one who signs off. Approvals and go/no-go calls that someone is blocked on.

Needs your input

A question or feedback they need back from you. Something to answer, not just approve.

FYI

Awareness only. Worth seeing, nothing owed - so it never crowds out what does need you.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it read my whole inbox?
No. Daybreak only surfaces messages a sender deliberately tagged for you. Everything else stays out of the queue entirely.
Where does my data go?
Nowhere. Daybreak has no server. It reads your tagged mail directly from Microsoft 365, inside Outlook, and shows it to you - nothing is copied out or stored.
How do I get it?
Your Microsoft 365 admin deploys it to your organization from the admin center. After that it just appears in Outlook for everyone in scope - there's nothing to download or install yourself.
How do people tag a message?
One dropdown in the Outlook compose window. The sender picks Decision, Input, or FYI, optionally adds a deadline, and sends as usual - no extra steps for you.
Which version of Outlook?
The new Outlook for Windows and Mac, and Outlook on the web. It shows up as a pinned side panel you can keep open beside your mail.

Bring it to your team

There's no installer and no app store. Daybreak is a hosted add-in - your admin gives the Microsoft 365 admin center its manifest link (below), and Outlook loads it for everyone from there.

  1. 1
    In the Microsoft 365 admin center, open Settings → Integrated apps → Upload custom apps.
  2. 2
    Choose Provide link to a manifest file and paste Daybreak's link.
  3. 3
    Assign it to your people and deploy. It appears in their Outlook - the tag button and their own queue.
